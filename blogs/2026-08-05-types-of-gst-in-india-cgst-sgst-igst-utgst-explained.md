Summary: India has four types of GST: CGST, SGST, IGST and UTGST, which apply based on the nature and location of a supply. This guide explains their full forms, differences, applicability and how to determine which type of GST applies to a transaction. Quick Overview Not all GST invoices are structured the same way.
